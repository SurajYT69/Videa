Name and logo concepts

Five names from the world of going to the cinema, each with its own mark. Lettering is a system font for now; the chosen one gets drawn letterforms.

UsherThe person with the torch who walks you to your seat: you give a title, it takes you there
DimlyThe house lights going down: a lamp that is half lit, half already dark
StubThe torn ticket you keep: a stub with its perforation
MatineeThe afternoon showing: a sun coming up behind a cinema screen
VelvetThe curtain before the film: two drapes parting
